繁體中文

「山靜」（以下稱「本錶面」）是由 Marx Tseng 開發的錶面，提供 Wear OS 與 Garmin Connect IQ 兩個版本。Wear OS 版是純資源的 Watch Face Format 錶面，不包含可執行程式碼；Garmin 版是在手錶上執行的 Connect IQ 錶面程式，詳見下方「Garmin Connect IQ 版」。

我們不收集任何資料

  • 本錶面不收集、不儲存、也不傳輸任何個人資料或使用資料。
  • 不需要帳號或登入；不使用網路連線，沒有伺服器。
  • 不含廣告、分析、當機回報或任何第三方追蹤工具。
  • Wear OS 版不要求感測器、位置、相簿、聯絡人或網路權限。Garmin 版只要求「感測器歷史記錄」權限，用於在手錶上顯示數值，不要求定位或網路通訊權限。

Wear OS：資訊位置（complication）的資料

步數、心率、天氣、電量等資訊由 Wear OS 系統或您自行選擇的第三方資料來源提供，本錶面僅在手錶上顯示，不會讀取後另存、上傳或分享。各資料來源如何處理資料，依該來源提供者的隱私權政策。心率等數值僅供顯示，不作醫療或診斷用途。

Wear OS：「我的相片」背景

個人相片由 Wear OS 系統與手機上的伴侶 App 讓您選取並傳送到手錶，僅保存在您的裝置上。本錶面不會存取您的相簿、不會把相片上傳到任何伺服器，也不會與任何人分享。

Wear OS：捷徑

點日期或時間只是開啟手錶上已安裝的 Google 日曆或 Google 時鐘；本錶面不會傳送任何資料給這些 App。

Garmin Connect IQ 版

山靜的 Garmin 版本是以 Monkey C 撰寫、在手錶上執行的 Connect IQ 錶面程式（目前支援 Venu 4）。本錶面沒有申請網路通訊（Communications）權限，無法連上網際網路，也不會把任何資料傳給開發者或第三方。

以下資料只在手錶上讀取並顯示，不會被儲存或傳出：

  • 感測器歷史記錄（Sensor History）權限：讀取最近的心率、身體能量指數（Body Battery）與壓力數值。只有在您選擇顯示這些資料時，才用於錶面上的對應欄位。
  • 活動資料：步數、卡路里、距離、爬升樓層、熱血時間，直接取自手錶本身的活動追蹤。
  • 天氣：氣溫，以及依手錶天氣資料附帶的觀測地點計算出的日出／日落時間。兩者都來自手錶已經透過 Garmin Connect 取得的天氣資料。本錶面沒有申請定位（Positioning）權限，不會自行讀取 GPS。
  • 電量、時間、日期、12／24 小時與公制／英制設定：來自手錶系統設定。

您選擇的背景、配色與資料欄位，由 Garmin 的 Connect IQ 系統儲存在手錶上。在 Connect IQ 或 Garmin Connect App 中修改時，會透過 Garmin 自己的同步機制傳到手錶。這些設定只包含選項編號，不含個人資料。

錶面的下載、安裝、更新與設定同步，都由 Garmin 的 Connect IQ Store 與 Garmin Connect 處理，適用 Garmin 隱私權政策。心率、壓力、身體能量指數等數值僅供參考，不作醫療或診斷用途。

兒童

本錶面並非專為兒童設計，也不會收集任何人的資料，包括兒童。

English

Shan Jing ("the watch face") is a watch face developed by Marx Tseng, available for Wear OS and Garmin Connect IQ. The Wear OS version is a resource-only Watch Face Format watch face and contains no executable code. The Garmin version is a Connect IQ watch face that runs on the watch; see "Garmin Connect IQ version" below.

We collect no data

  • The watch face does not collect, store or transmit any personal or usage data.
  • No account or sign-in is required. It does not use the network and has no servers.
  • It contains no ads, analytics, crash reporting or other third-party tracking.
  • The Wear OS version requests no sensor, location, photo library, contacts or network permissions. The Garmin version requests only the Sensor History permission, to display values on the watch. It requests no location or communications permission.

Wear OS: complication data

Steps, heart rate, weather, battery and other complication data are provided by the Wear OS system or by third-party data sources that you choose. The watch face only displays this data on your watch; it does not save, upload or share it. Each data source handles data according to its own provider's privacy policy. Heart rate and similar values are shown for display only and are not intended for medical or diagnostic use.

Wear OS: "My Photo" background

Your photo is selected and transferred to the watch by the Wear OS system and the companion app on your phone, and stays on your devices. The watch face does not access your photo library, does not upload the photo to any server and does not share it with anyone.

Wear OS: shortcuts

Tapping the date or time simply opens Google Calendar or Google Clock if installed on your watch. The watch face sends no data to those apps.

Garmin Connect IQ version

The Garmin version of Shan Jing is a Connect IQ watch face written in Monkey C and run on the watch (currently Venu 4). It does not request the Communications permission, cannot access the internet, and sends no data to the developer or any third party.

The following data is read and shown on the watch only. It is never stored or sent anywhere:

  • Sensor History permission: reads recent heart rate, Body Battery and stress values. They are used only for the matching data slot, and only when you choose to show it.
  • Activity data: steps, calories, distance, floors climbed and intensity minutes from the watch's own activity tracking.
  • Weather: temperature, and sunrise/sunset times calculated from the observation location included in the watch's weather data. Both come from weather data the watch already receives through Garmin Connect. The watch face does not request the Positioning permission and never reads GPS itself.
  • Battery, time, date, 12/24-hour and unit settings from the watch's system settings.

Your chosen background, theme and data slots are stored on the watch by the Connect IQ system. If you change them in the Connect IQ or Garmin Connect app, they are delivered through Garmin's own sync. They contain option numbers only, no personal data.

Downloading, installing, updating and syncing settings for the watch face are handled by the Garmin Connect IQ Store and Garmin Connect, under Garmin's privacy policy. Heart rate, stress, Body Battery and other health values are for information only and are not intended for medical or diagnostic use.

Children

The watch face is not designed for children and collects no data from anyone, including children.
