繁體中文

「星艦橋 Deckline」（以下稱「本錶面」）是由 Marx Tseng 開發的科幻儀表風格錶面，提供 Wear OS 與 Garmin Connect IQ 兩個版本。Wear OS 版是純資源的 Watch Face Format 錶面，不包含可執行程式碼；Garmin 版是在手錶上執行的 Connect IQ 錶面程式，詳見下方「Garmin Connect IQ 版」。

我們不收集任何資料

  • 本錶面不收集、不儲存、也不傳輸任何個人資料或使用資料。
  • 不需要帳號或登入；不使用網路連線，沒有伺服器。
  • 不含廣告、分析、當機回報或任何第三方追蹤工具。
  • Wear OS 版不要求任何權限（感測器、位置、相簿、聯絡人或網路皆無）。Garmin 版只要求「感測器歷史記錄」權限，用於在手錶上顯示心率，不要求定位或網路通訊權限。

Wear OS：資訊位置（complication）的資料

心率、步數、天氣、電量等資訊由 Wear OS 系統或您自行選擇的第三方資料來源提供，本錶面僅在手錶上顯示（包含兩側量表依資料來源提供的範圍或目標顯示進度），不會讀取後另存、上傳或分享。各資料來源如何處理資料，依該來源提供者的隱私權政策。心率等數值僅供顯示，不作醫療或診斷用途。

購買

Google Play 上的付費下載由 Google Play 處理，適用 Google 隱私權政策；開發者不會取得您的付款資訊。

Garmin Connect IQ 版

星艦橋的 Garmin 版本是以 Monkey C 撰寫、在手錶上執行的 Connect IQ 錶面程式（圓形 AMOLED 機種）。本錶面沒有申請網路通訊（Communications）權限，無法連上網際網路，也不會把任何資料傳給開發者或第三方。

以下資料只在手錶上讀取並顯示，不會被儲存或傳出：

  • 感測器歷史記錄（Sensor History）權限：讀取最近的心率，只在您選擇顯示心率時使用。
  • 活動資料：步數、卡路里、距離、爬升樓層、每週活動分鐘數，直接取自手錶本身的活動追蹤。
  • 天氣：氣溫，以及依手錶天氣資料附帶的觀測地點計算出的日出／日落時間，皆來自手錶已經透過 Garmin Connect 取得的天氣資料。本錶面沒有申請定位（Positioning）權限，不會自行讀取 GPS。
  • 電量、時間、日期、12／24 小時與公制／英制設定：來自手錶系統設定。

您選擇的版面、時間樣式、主題與資料欄位，由 Garmin 的 Connect IQ 系統儲存在手錶上；在 Connect IQ 或 Garmin Connect App 中修改時，透過 Garmin 自己的同步機制傳到手錶。這些設定只包含選項編號，不含個人資料。

錶面的下載、安裝、更新與設定同步，都由 Garmin 的 Connect IQ Store 與 Garmin Connect 處理，適用 Garmin 隱私權政策。心率等數值僅供參考，不作醫療或診斷用途。

兒童

本錶面並非專為兒童設計，也不會收集任何人的資料，包括兒童。

English

Deckline ("the watch face") is a sci-fi dashboard watch face developed by Marx Tseng, available for Wear OS and Garmin Connect IQ. The Wear OS version is a resource-only Watch Face Format watch face and contains no executable code. The Garmin version is a Connect IQ watch face that runs on the watch; see "Garmin Connect IQ version" below.

We collect no data

  • The watch face does not collect, store or transmit any personal or usage data.
  • No account or sign-in is required. It does not use the network and has no servers.
  • It contains no ads, analytics, crash reporting or other third-party tracking.
  • The Wear OS version requests no permissions at all (no sensors, location, photos, contacts or network). The Garmin version requests only the Sensor History permission, to show heart rate on the watch. It requests no location or communications permission.

Wear OS: complication data

Heart rate, steps, weather, battery and other complication data are provided by the Wear OS system or by third-party data sources that you choose. The watch face only displays this data on your watch (including the side gauges, which show progress using the range or goal supplied by the data source); it does not save, upload or share it. Each data source handles data according to its own provider's privacy policy. Heart rate and similar values are shown for display only and are not intended for medical or diagnostic use.

Purchases

Paid downloads on Google Play are processed by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Garmin Connect IQ version

The Garmin version of Deckline is a Connect IQ watch face written in Monkey C and run on the watch (round AMOLED models). It does not request the Communications permission, cannot access the internet, and sends no data to the developer or any third party.

The following data is read and shown on the watch only. It is never stored or sent anywhere:

  • Sensor History permission: reads your recent heart rate, used only when you choose to show heart rate.
  • Activity data: steps, calories, distance, floors climbed and weekly active minutes from the watch's own activity tracking.
  • Weather: temperature, and sunrise/sunset times calculated from the observation location included in the watch's weather data, all from weather data the watch already receives through Garmin Connect. The watch face does not request the Positioning permission and never reads GPS itself.
  • Battery, time, date, 12/24-hour and unit settings from the watch's system settings.

Your chosen layout, time style, theme and data slots are stored on the watch by the Connect IQ system. If you change them in the Connect IQ or Garmin Connect app, they are delivered through Garmin's own sync. They contain option numbers only, no personal data.

Downloading, installing, updating and syncing settings for the watch face are handled by the Garmin Connect IQ Store and Garmin Connect, under Garmin's privacy policy. Heart rate and other health values are for information only and are not intended for medical or diagnostic use.

Children

The watch face is not designed for children and collects no data from anyone, including children.
